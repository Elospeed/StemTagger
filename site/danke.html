Your first start

  1. UnzipUnzip StemTagger.zip into any folder and start StemTagger.exe. No installation needed.
  2. Windows warns?If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
  3. Drop stem files or MP3sFill in title, artist and cover, then click Save. Audio, stems and Traktor data stay unchanged.
